Counted in eights

Dance teachers count in eights, and so does RUKH. One round from switch-on to the clip:

  1. 1Open /screen on the TVa room code and a QR appear
  2. 2Scan the QR with the phoneor type the 4-letter code
  3. 3Lean the phone on the wall2–4 m away, sideways
  4. 4Pick a danceeasy, medium or hard
  5. 5 · 6 · 7 · 8Count-in, then 30 seconds of dancingmoves and beat are scored live for every dancer; the TV hands out a clip by QR

Two ways to play

Both use the same phone and the same TV. Switch between them on the TV screen.

Battle

Up to four dancers side by side, each on their own colour of tape. Points for every move that matches the reference and every accent on the beat; a podium, tonight’s top list and a clip for the crew.

Ties share the place. A dancer who leaves the frame keeps their points.

Lesson

The same dance cut into 4–6 moves of 8 counts. Learn one move at a time, then put the routine together.

Open it from the TV screen: the phone stays in the same room.

Dances

Each dance is a real dancer on video with our own music, made on the beat grid of the moves.

For your event

  • Any TV, projector or laptop on HDMI, plus one phone — no sensors, no install.
  • Rounds of 30 seconds keep a queue moving; tonight’s top list keeps people coming back.
  • Every crew leaves with a clip of their round on their own phone.
