Get the battleon the floor

RUKH needs two screens in one room: a phone that films and a TV, laptop or tablet that counts. Setup takes about two minutes. Below is the setup in eight counts, what the scores mean and fixes for the usual snags.

1

Open the screen

On a smart TV browser or on a laptop connected by HDMI, open rukh.mmix.biz/screen. In the RUKH app, tap Open the TV screen on a tablet. The screen shows a 4-character room code and a QR code.

Turn the sound up: the music carries the count. With a keyboard, ↑ and ↓ pick the dance, Enter starts the round, Esc ends it early, M flips the mirror.

2

Stand the phone up

  • Prop it against a wall or on a stand, landscape, at waist to chest height, 3–4 m from the dancers.
  • Everyone should be in frame from head to feet with a little room to move. Up to four dancers; each gets a tape colour: pink, cyan, lime, orange.
  • Light the dancers from the front. A bright window behind them makes bodies hard to find.
  • Plug the phone in for long sessions. Body tracking keeps the processor busy.
3

Pair the phone with the screen

In the app, tap Be the camera for a TV and scan the QR on the screen or type the code. In a phone browser, open rukh.mmix.biz/cam and type the code. Allow the camera when asked.

The phone shows LIVE when the screen sees it. Same Wi-Fi gives the smoothest video. Different networks work too: RUKH then sends the picture through its relay, a little less smooth, with the same scoring.

4

Count in and dance

Pick a dance and start. The screen counts you in on the beat, "5 · 6 · 7 · 8", then plays the reference dancer. Follow the moves and stay in frame; the 8-count ruler under the video shows where you are in the phrase.

No TV at hand? Practice solo in the app runs the whole thing on one phone.

5

What the scores mean

MATCH
How close your arms, legs and body line are to the reference at that moment, 0–100. Mirror mode is on by default, so you can copy the dancer like a mirror.
ON BEAT
Whether your sharpest moves land on the beat of the track: ON BEAT, EARLY or LATE.
SHARP · SOLID · LOOSE · LOST
The judge's word for the match, from best to "find the dancer again".
POINTS
Every beat adds points for the match and a bonus for hitting the beat. The winner is the one with the most points when the music stops.

"Tonight's top" on the screen keeps the best rounds on that device until you clear its data.

6

Take the clip home

After the round the screen shows a QR code. Scan it to watch and download the round clip with skeletons and scores. The link works for 7 days, then the clip is deleted. To delete it sooner, send us the link; details in the privacy policy.

7

Fixes for the usual snags

"Camera blocked" on the phone

Allow the camera for RUKH: in the app, phone Settings → Apps → RUKH → Permissions (Android) or Settings → RUKH → Camera (iPhone). In a browser, tap the lock icon next to the address and allow the camera, then reload.

The phone never shows LIVE

Check that the code on the phone matches the screen. Reload the screen page, then reopen the camera page. Both devices need internet; the screen and the phone may be on different networks.

Video on the TV is choppy

Put both devices on the same Wi-Fi so the video can go directly. Move the phone closer to the router, or close other apps on it.

A dancer is not detected

Step back until head and feet are in frame, add light from the front, and avoid baggy clothes in the same colour as the wall. Four dancers is the maximum.

ON BEAT stays low

Turn the music up so everyone hears it, and hit the beat with clear moves: arms and steps that stop on the count. A slow phone can lag; plug it in and close other apps.

The clip QR says it could not save

The screen needs internet at the end of the round. Wait a minute and play the next round. Very many rounds in a short time are paused for a while to stop abuse.
